What the game sends (version 1.1.1 and later)

Anonymous usage statistics. The game sends short gameplay events to our own server, for example “career started” with the home country and difficulty, “player signed”, “deal accepted on the second try”, “event answered with option 2” or “season ended with 6 clients”. Each event holds numbers and fixed labels chosen by the game, plus the app version, the operating system and its version, your phone’s language and time zone offset, and the country of the connection.

  • Events are tied only to a random install ID made on your phone (and a random ID per session). It is not your name, not your Apple or Google account, not a device identifier and not the advertising identifier. A new random ID replaces it after 13 months.
  • Your agent’s and agency’s names, and anything else you type, are never part of an event. Nor are the names of players or clubs.
  • The server derives the country from the connection; it never stores or logs your IP address.
  • Raw events are deleted after 120 days. Daily totals that hold no IDs are kept for 25 months.
  • Offline, events wait on the phone (at most 1,000) and are sent later. The game works fully without a connection.
  • Turn it off any time: in the game, open Settings and switch off “Share anonymous stats”. Off deletes the events still waiting and nothing more is sent. If you had switched analytics off in an earlier version, it stays off.

Anonymous error reports. If the game hits a software error, it sends an “error” event with the kind of error, a short fingerprint, and the names of the game’s own code files and lines where it happened, so we can find and fix the bug. The error’s message is never sent (it could contain something you typed), and nor is anything else from your phone. These reports go through the same anonymous statistics, with the same install ID, retention and off switch.

Where the install came from (iPhone only). On first launch the game asks Apple’s AdServices framework for a token and sends it to our server once, so we can learn whether the install came from an Apple Search Ads campaign (campaign, ad group and keyword, the country and the date). This is Apple’s privacy-preserving attribution: it contains no advertising identifier and nothing about you, it is not tracking, and the game shows no tracking prompt. If statistics are off, nothing is sent.

“Write to us”. If you send us a message from the game’s settings, we receive the message, its type (bug, idea or other), the app and system details above, the install ID (so we can match a bug to what happened), and your email address only if you choose to give one so we can reply. Messages are kept for 24 months. This works even when statistics are off, because you send it on purpose.

Our server runs on Cloudflare, with the database in the European Union. Cloudflare processes the data for us as a service provider. Versions 1.0 to 1.1.0 sent nothing at all.

What we never collect

No name, no contacts, no location, no photos, no microphone, no camera, no advertising identifier and no device identifier. The game contains no advertising libraries, no third-party analytics or crash reporting service and no social or sign-in integrations.

What the app stores on your device

Your agency, clients, clubs and progress are stored locally on your phone or tablet. We do not upload them to our servers. Your device’s own backup (iCloud / Google backup) may include them, depending on your system settings. Small files in the app’s support folder hold the random install ID, your statistics setting and events waiting to be sent.

Advertising and purchases

There are no ads and no in-app purchases in Pitch Agent 27, and no advertising SDKs are included. If that ever changes, we will update this policy and tell you in the game before it applies.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent data.
  • We do not share data with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) and we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone. The statistics hold nothing that identifies a person.

Deleting your data

  • Save games: delete them in the game, or uninstall the app / clear the app data.
  • Deleting the app deletes your save, the install ID and any events still waiting on the phone.
  • Turning statistics off stops all sending at once.
  • To have the statistics and messages from your install deleted from our server, email us. We will help you find your install and erase it.

Your rights

If you are in the EU/EEA or UK, you have rights under the GDPR (access, deletion, objection, complaint to a supervisory authority). Since we hold no personal data, there is usually little to hand over, but feel free to ask: vibalyze@zohomail.eu.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above and, for important changes, tell you in the game.
