Pitch Agent 27 is an offline game. We built it to collect as little as possible. Here is exactly what happens with data.

Who we are

Pitch Agent 27 is made and published by Vibalyze OÜ, Järvevana tee 9, 11314 Tallinn, Estonia (registry code 17240903). Vibalyze OÜ is the controller for the optional anonymous analytics described below. You can reach us at vibalyze@zohomail.eu.

What we collect

No personal data. There are no accounts, no logins, no sign-up. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, location, photos or any other personal information.

Your save games stay on your device. Your agency, clients, clubs and progress are stored locally on your phone or tablet. We do not upload them to our servers. Your device’s own backup (iCloud / Google backup) may include them, depending on your system settings.

Optional anonymous analytics. To find bugs and see which parts of the game are fun or confusing, the game can send a few anonymous gameplay events (for example “app opened”, “career started” with the chosen country and difficulty, “season ended”, weeks played) to PostHog, hosted in the European Union.

  • Events are tied only to a random install ID generated on your device. It is not linked to your identity, your Apple/Google account or any advertising ID.
  • We do not collect your name, email or contacts. Like any internet request, the connection to PostHog reveals your IP address; we do not use it to identify you or build a location profile.
  • If you play without an internet connection, nothing is sent.
  • You can switch analytics off at any time in Settings inside the game (“Share anonymous usage data”). When it is off, no analytics events are sent.

Advertising

There are no ads in Pitch Agent 27 at launch, and no advertising SDKs are included. If that ever changes, we will update this policy and tell you in the game before it applies.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent data.
  • We do not share data with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) and we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone. Because the game collects no personal data, there is nothing that identifies a child.

Deleting your data

  • Save games: delete them in the game, or uninstall the app / clear the app data.
  • Analytics: turn analytics off in Settings. Uninstalling resets the random install ID. If you want your analytics events deleted, email us and we will help you identify and remove them.

Your rights

If you are in the EU/EEA or UK, you have rights under the GDPR (access, deletion, objection, complaint to a supervisory authority). Since we hold no personal data, there is usually nothing to hand over, but feel free to ask: vibalyze@zohomail.eu.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above and, for important changes, tell you in the game.
