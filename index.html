Pitch Agent 27

Scout wonderkids, close the deals, own the club.

Pitch Agent 27 is a football agent tycoon game for iOS and Android. Build your agency, sign young talent, negotiate transfers and contracts, and grow from a one-person office into the most powerful name in the game.

The game runs offline on your device. There is no account and no sign-in, and your save stays on your phone.
